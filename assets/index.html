Instead of a traditional villain,
this version is a master thief who uses her elite criminal
training to stop V.I.L.E. (Villains' International
League of Evil)
and return stolen goods to their rightful owners.

Mission

Stealing from V.I.L.E.:: She uses the training they gave her
to intercept their high-stakes heists before they can successfully
fence or hoard stolen goods.

Allies and Enemies

Player: Her brilliant hacker friend who guides her through heists and serves as her tech support.
Zack and Ivy: A pair of Boston-born siblings who act as her trusty ground team and sidekicks.
ACME & Interpol: Law enforcement agencies that constantly chase her, mistakenly believing she is a regular criminal rather than a vigilante
